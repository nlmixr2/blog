## Email

**Subject:** nlmixr2scm is on CRAN: stepwise covariate modeling for nlmixr2 fits

Hi all,

If you've built covariate models in NONMEM, you've probably used `scm` in
Perl-speaks-NONMEM (PsN). `nlmixr2scm`, now on CRAN, brings the same
stepwise search (Jonsson & Karlsson, 1998) to nlmixr2. It works directly on
a fit you already have, so there are no control streams or configuration
files to write.

```r
install.packages("nlmixr2scm")
```

Today's nlmixr2 blog post, written by Justin Wilkins, walks through it:

- **Forward inclusion, then stricter backward elimination.** Both phases
  use likelihood-ratio tests (p < 0.05 forward, p < 0.01 backward by
  default), as in PsN.
- **One call does the setup.** `runSCM()` takes the original, untransformed
  data, centers continuous covariates (at the median or a value you choose),
  expands categorical covariates into indicators, and writes each candidate
  into the model for you.
- **A worked warfarin example.** Six candidates for weight and sex on
  clearance and volume are tested, and the search ends with weight on
  volume. `summary()` compares the base, forward and backward models side
  by side, and the final model is an ordinary nlmixr2 fit.
- **Built for real searches.** Every candidate fit is saved, so an
  interrupted run resumes where it stopped. Candidates within a step fit
  in parallel, checked against your core count. Stalled or implausible
  fits are refit automatically.

Read the post: https://blog.nlmixr2.org/blog/2026-10-23-nlmixr2scm/

Or watch the narrated walkthrough: VIDEO_URL

Justin Wilkins and Yaping Liu did most of the work on this package, with
contributions from Bill Denney, Vipul Mann, Vishal Sarsani and Christian
Bartels. The design owes a great deal to the original PsN `scm` and the
people who built it. Bug reports and feature requests are welcome at
https://github.com/nlmixr2/nlmixr2scm/issues.
