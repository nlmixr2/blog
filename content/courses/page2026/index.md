## Workshop overview 

Bill Denney, Anne Keunecke, Theo Papathanasiou, Giuseppe Pasculli, Zhonghui Huang and Justin Wilkins will present a course on simulation and parameter estimation using `rxode2` and `nlmixr2` at PAGE 2026 in Dubrovnik, Croatia, on 2 June 2026 from 13.00-17.00 CET.
